# Help: I cannot find an issue report

Intended visibility: PUBLIC. Source version: 1. Authored: 2026-10-06.

## What to provide

Tell support approximately when you submitted the report, whether you received
a report ID or an error, and whether you later archived it. Share the report ID
if you have it. Do not share passwords or access tokens.

## What the behavior means

A successful submission returns a report ID. A validation error means that
attempt was rejected. Your list contains non-archived reports for your current
customer account. Archived reports no longer appear, and restoring them is not
currently supported.

## When the outcome is unclear

Clicking submit alone does not confirm that a report was saved. A not-found
result alone does not explain why a report is unavailable. Support may need to
investigate. Do not repeatedly submit the same report solely because the first
response was lost; the outcome of that attempt may still be unknown.
