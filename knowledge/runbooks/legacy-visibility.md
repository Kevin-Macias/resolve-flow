## Historical advice

to locate an archived issue report and select “Restore” to return it to the list.
That was the legacy response to “I submitted a report but cannot find it.”

## Current limitation

parameter or restore operation. Do not present these historical steps as a current
solution. The matching symptom and keywords do not establish applicability.
